Common questions

How far ahead should I book?

For summer weekends and weddings, book 2–3 months ahead if you can. Weekday and smaller events can often be done with a week or two of notice. Call us. We'll always try.

Is there a minimum number of guests?

It depends on the menu. Drop-off lunches start smaller. Lobster bakes and pig roasts need a larger group to make sense. Tell us your headcount and we'll suggest what fits.

Can you handle allergies and dietary restrictions?

Yes. Gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan and dairy-free options are marked on the menu, and we plan around allergies when you book. Let us know on the quote form.

Where do you cater?

We're based at 95 Darling Ave in South Portland and cater throughout Greater Portland and Southern Maine, including Casco Bay islands like Peaks Island.

Do you provide staff, setup and cleanup?

We offer drop-off, buffet and full-service options. Full service includes setup, serving and cleanup of our cooking and serving area.

How is pricing worked out?

Every event is quoted on guest count, menu, service style and location. We work to your budget. Ask, and we'll show you options at different price points.
