      Good to know

      How do I order take out?

      Call the store at (207) 693-8840 or stop in, Wednesday through Sunday from 11am to 6pm. Prices on the take out menu include Maine sales tax.

      How many people does a family-size portion feed?

      Each family-size portion is about 4 servings. Mix and match meats, mac & cheese, beans and coleslaw.

      How big are the catering platters?

      Party platters, boards and grazes serve about 10–12 people each.

      Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

      Yes. Our homemade black bean burger is gluten-free and vegan, and you can upgrade to a gluten-free Udi's bun. For catering, tell us about dietary needs in your inquiry and we'll plan around them.

      Do you do lobster bakes, pig roasts and weddings?

      Yes. Those menus are put together per event. Send an inquiry or call for menus and availability.

      How is catering priced?

      Catering is quoted per event, based on your menu, guest count and service style. The lobster roll is market price.
