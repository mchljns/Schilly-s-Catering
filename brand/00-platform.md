# Schilly’s: Brand platform

This page decides what the identity has to do before any direction gets drawn. Every route after this is judged against it.

## The platform

**What it is.** Schilly’s Take Out & Catering Kitchen, 224 Roosevelt Trail (Route 302), Casco, Maine. Take out, family meals and catering. Wednesday to Sunday, 11 am to 6 pm.

**Who it’s for.**
- First: people picking up dinner or lunch on Route 302. They check if it’s open, what it costs, and call.
- Second: people planning an event (a wedding, an office lunch, a backyard party). They scan the catering menu, then ask.

**The tension.** A painted roadside sign of a logo, and a menu that runs from a $5 hot dog to a wedding charcuterie board. The identity has to feel like a takeout counter and still be trusted with a wedding.

**The brand’s role.** Show the food and the facts, then get out of the way. The logo and the photos carry the personality, and the page carries the information.

**Promise** (their words). Slow smoked favorites, homemade comfort food & Maine hospitality.

**Personality.** Homemade, generous, plain-spoken, a bit roadside. Never slick, never rustic-cute (no chalkboard fonts, no fake wood textures, no mason jars).

**Voice.** Schilly’s own words only. Capitals for headings, bullets between items, short friendly lines ("HUNGRY YET?").

## Where the identity actually lives

| Place | What the brand gets there | What that demands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phone, first screen | One screen, often in a car or at work | Hours, open now, phone and address without scrolling far |
| The menu | The longest read on the site | Prices that scan like a printed menu |
| Link preview in a text or post | A small card | Logo and food photo, readable at 300 px wide |
| Instagram (@schillyseats) avatar | A small circle | The sign survives at 110 px, the wordmark doesn’t need to |
| Printed take-out menu | A sheet on the counter or a fridge | Two pages, black and red print still works |
| Catering inquiry | A decision someone has to defend to a boss or a couple | Calm, credible, complete form |

## The test: eight criteria

Every route must pass all eight, shown on the same mockups.

1. **Open, call, go.** On a 390 px phone, hours, open status, phone and address on the first screen.
2. **Menu scans like paper.** Name and price read as one line. A price is two taps away.
3. **Their words only.** Every sentence traces to schillyscatering.com.
4. **Logo untouched.** The sign is used as supplied.
5. **Color in order.** One ground, one structure color, accents under 5% and never side by side at equal size (`color/README.md`).
6. **Credible for a wedding.** The catering view could be shown to a couple without embarrassment.
7. **Ownable on Route 302.** Doesn’t look like a delivery app, a chain, or the competitors listed in `01-strategy.md`.
8. **Easy to keep.** The owner can change a price in one file and print a menu from the page.
