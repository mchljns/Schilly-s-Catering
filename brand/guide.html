Brand guide

How Schilly’s looks on the website, social posts and printed menus. Every color comes from the logo. Every sentence on the website comes from Schilly’s. Rules and reasoning: brand/README.md.

Logo

The logo is painted artwork. Use the supplied files as they are.

On Cream. The default.

On Plank.

On Frame Navy.

  1. Minimum width 150 px on screens. Below that the ribbon lettering can’t be read.
  2. Clear space on every side: the height of the “Kitchen” script, about one sixth of the logo height.
  3. Never retype, trace, recolor, crop, stretch or animate it. No added shadow or outline.
  4. On photos only in the link-preview card, where its own navy frame separates it from the photo.

Type

Oswald and Lato, the same faces as Schilly’s current site. Both are open-licensed and self-hosted.

Smoked. Homemade. Maine.

Oswald 600, uppercase, tracking 0.02em. Headings, section titles, buttons, prices.

Slow smoked favorites, homemade comfort food & Maine hospitality. Lato 700 for item names.

Lato 400 and 700. Body 18 px, line height 1.6. Details 16 px. Never below 14 px.

Buttons and shape

View Our Menu Print menu (207)693-8840
  1. One control height: 48 px. Touch targets at least 44 px, 40 px for compact chips.
  2. 6 px corners on buttons, inputs, cards and photos, after the sign’s rounded corners. No pills.
  3. Hover and focus change color only. No lift, slide or scroll animation.

Photography

Schilly’s own photos only. No stock food.

Words

  1. Use Schilly’s own wording. The saved copy of their site is in brand/source/.
  2. New interface labels go in brand/source/ui-labels.txt: three words or fewer, and they name the action.
  3. No invented reviews, prices, awards, promises or FAQ answers.
  4. No emojis, gradients, stock icons or decorative card grids.
