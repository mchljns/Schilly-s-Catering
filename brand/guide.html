BRAND GUIDE

How Schilly’s looks on the website, social posts and printed menus. Every color comes from the logo, and every sentence on the website comes from Schilly’s. Reasoning: 00-platform.md through 05-identity-system.md.

Logo

On Cream. The default.

On Plank.

On Frame Navy: avoid. The sign’s frame merges into the ground.

  1. Minimum width 150 px on screens. Below that the ribbon lettering can’t be read.
  2. Clear space on every side: the height of the “Kitchen” script, about one sixth of the logo height.
  3. Never retype, trace, recolor, crop, stretch or animate it. No added shadow or outline.
