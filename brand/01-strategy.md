# Schilly’s: Strategy

## Positioning

For people on Route 302 and around the Lakes Region who want real smoked food without a sit-down meal, Schilly’s is the take-out and catering kitchen in Casco that does slow smoked favorites and homemade comfort food, from a $5 hot dog to a catered wedding.

The line that proves it is theirs: **SMOKED. HOMEMADE. MAINE.**

## What sets it apart (from their own menus)

- Take out and catering from one kitchen. Family-size portions bridge the two.
- A real catering range: boxed lunches, soups and crocks, nineteen platters and grazes.
- Smoked meats across both menus: pulled pork, pulled chicken, brisket.

## Brand architecture

1. **Master brand.** The sign logo. Quiet everywhere else.
2. **Two menus, one kitchen.** Take Out (priced, everyday) and Catering (events, inquiry). Same type and color, different jobs: Take Out leads with price, Catering leads with the + Add and inquiry.
3. **Events.** Lobster Bakes, Pig Roasts and Weddings sit under Catering until Schilly’s publishes those menus.

## Design brief for the directions

Two routes, both built only from the logo’s parts (navy frame, red ribbon, yellow lettering, wood plank). They differ in how much of the sign the page wears.

- **A. Menu Board.** The page as a printed menu: cream paper, navy type, hairline rules around section titles, dotted leaders to prices. The logo is the only loud thing. Red and yellow are rare.
- **B. Roadside Sign.** The page wears the sign: navy bands, the red ribbon as a recurring device for section labels, yellow on navy for the headline and the main action. More color, more 302.
